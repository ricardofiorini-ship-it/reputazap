What you sell

Products and a platform, in one package.

Every device you sell comes connected to the StarTouch platform. That is what makes your offer different, and what keeps your customers coming back for more units.

The hardware

  • NFC review card, credit-card size, for the staff and the cashier.
  • Counter plaques M and G (10 × 15 cm and 15 × 20 cm), 2 mm black PS with an angled base.
  • NFC + QR Code on every unit: it works with any modern iPhone or Android, and the QR Code covers the rest.
  • A unique code per unit, programmed and checked before shipping.

The platform

  • Free dashboard for every business: Google rating, latest reviews and how many taps each device brings.
  • Competitor view: how the business compares with the businesses around it on Google.
  • Per-device tracking: owners give a card to each staff member and see who brings the most reviews.
  • Smart Menu (Pro plan): one tap opens a page with the review, the menu, Wi-Fi, social links and more.
  • Available in English, Spanish, Chinese and Portuguese.

FAQ

Questions distributors ask.

Does it work in my country?

Yes, wherever businesses have a Google Business Profile. The device opens the Google review screen of the business it is linked to, in any country, and the customer reviews in their own language.

Do my customers pay a monthly fee?

No. The device works for life and the dashboard is free. The Pro plan, with the Smart Menu, is optional.

Can I sell under my own brand?

The products carry the StarTouch brand ("Powered by StarTouch"). Co-branding for larger volumes can be discussed in the agreement.

What do I need to start?

A registered company, a sales channel to local businesses (agencies, POS suppliers, marketing consultants and retailers are a great fit) and a first order from US$ 500.

How fast do you reply?

Within 2 business days, by e-mail, with the price list and next steps.
